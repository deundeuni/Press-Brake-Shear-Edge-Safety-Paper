The integrated design authority of this architecture belongs to the designer (deundeuni).

   * Decelerated Descent Linked to Mute Point, Ultra-Low Latency Stop, and Immediate Upward Recovery Logic — Linked with EN 12622 slow speed (Safe Speed below 10mm/s) and Mute Point setting profiles, it includes a configuration that mitigates inertia and hydraulic shock, prevents upward recovery mechanism offset phenomena via bounce suppression, alleviates worker startle reflex, prevents musculoskeletal diseases from long-term repetitive vibration, and induces eFPGA 0.1ms cutoff control and upward recovery to a safe height (origin) upon detecting abnormal symptoms.
   * Continuous Quiet Assist Maintenance Logic during Shear Manual Machining under 10mm and Existing Safeguard Bypass/Muting — Includes a configuration where, even during small micro-part cutting or physical bypassing of existing optical sensors, the independent triple sensors and eFPGA-based hardware interlocks are not disabled, performing continuous monitoring and ultra-low latency cutoff as the 'final Quiet Assist'.

1. Structural Limitations, Empirical Failure Modes, and Potential Operational Risk Analysis of Press, Shear, and Press Brake Pinch Hazards
 * Official Pinch Point Machinery Classification by KISA and OSHA 1910.212/217 — Presses, shears (cutters), molding machines, and bending machines (press brakes) are designated as representative high-load pinch point and Point of Operation hazard machines in industrial sites, strictly requiring physical and electrical safety devices compliant with ANSI B11.3 and OSHA regulations.
 * Actual Failure Mode 1 — Accidents during Manual Work after Temporary Bypass of Safety Optical Sensors due to Micro-Cutting under 10mm and Alarm Fatigue — When performing remnant cutting under 10mm or small atypical processes on shears/press brakes, existing simple optical sensor systems frequently misjudge even normal operating fingers as hazards, causing frequent Nuisance Trips. This acts as the root cause of the practice where operators perform defenseless manual work with optical sensors physically bypassed or masked with tape, repeatedly leading to patterns where the cutting blade operates unexpectedly during work, resulting in finger amputation accidents.
 * Actual Failure Mode 2 — Free Fall by Self-Weight during Sudden Power Outages and Auto-Restart Malfunctions upon Power Recovery — A serious structural hazard pattern is reported where, if a momentary blackout occurs during press/press brake operation, the loss of hydraulic pressure causes the upper die to free-fall by its own weight, pressing the hydraulic cylinder, or where the auto-restart setting of the controller upon power recovery injures a worker who was cleaning or inspecting between the dies.
 * Actual Failure Mode 3 — Entanglement/Pinch Accidents caused by Night Shift Continuous Work Fatigue and Prolonged Accumulation of Shock/Vibration — During prolonged continuous work, the strong shock and vibration of the machine are fully transmitted to the worker's body, inducing musculoskeletal fatigue and concentration degradation. Accidents frequently occur where workers are entangled while attempting to remove jammed defective products by hand without shutting off the power.
 * Mitigation of Potential Operational Adverse Effects and Application of Modern Safety Governance — This system is not a tool to monitor and track worker behavior, but operates as a Quiet Auxiliary that occasionally cross-checks the worker's status and safe posture to provide an environmental foundation for secure operation. According to the soma-moa philosophy, by modernizing Heinrich's 1931 concept to apply Safety-II (flexibly supplementing the continuity of 9,999 normal tasks rather than just suppressing accidents) and Just Culture, it preemptively mitigates the risk of safety device neutralization caused by backlash against field surveillance.
 * Strengthening Institutional Responsibility and Practical Limitations in the Field — Although pinch hazard prevention is urgent under the Serious Accidents Punishment Act (Korea) and OSHA General Duty clauses, most sites face the burden of system reconstruction and operational downtime risks associated with replacing equipment with heavy, high-reliability machinery or entirely modifying the main PLC circuits.
2. Necessity of Autonomous Computation and Local Physical Control on Edge and On-Device Terminals
 * Non-Invasive Silent Auxiliary and Unused Terminal Linkage Structure — Complying with the IEC 60204-1 industrial machinery electrical equipment open standard, it avoids directly modifying the main equipment's PLC or main controller firmware. By directly connecting to unused safety option terminals left as spares by the manufacturer (e.g., X3/X4 spare slots), sniffing existing optical sensor OSSD photocouplers, or serially inserting Dry Contacts (non-powered contacts) into the emergency stop/safety relay loop, it aims for a lightweight structure where a lightweight independent module flexibly controls contact signals (Motor EN / Relay) only in emergencies.
 * Guaranteeing Independent Safety Assistance when Existing Safeguards are Bypassed/Muted — Even if the existing main optical sensors or interlock guards are manually bypassed or automatically muted to perform special processes on the shear/press brake, this non-invasive module operates independently of the main PLC's control state. It is the only system to continuously execute cutoff control upon a worker's physical hazard intrusion through triple sensor fusion and eFPGA interlocks.
 * Ultra-Low Latency Hardware Interlocks and Local AI Fusion — Without relying on upper-level clouds or external networks, it maximizes physical execution speed by cross-verifying sensor signals and AI inferences within the terminal's local eFPGA circuit and a lightweight local NPU terminal. (Oriented towards IEC 61508/62061 functional safety)
 * Non-Powered Magnetic/Mechanical Latch against Power Outages and Power Lockout upon Power Recovery (soma-moa L0/L1 Linkage) — Upon main system power loss, energy storage elements and non-powered electro-permanent magnets (EPM) / mechanical air brake latches operate immediately to physically attenuate and fix the free fall of the die. Upon power recovery, the soma-moa L2 FSM RECOVERY latch state is automatically maintained, applying a Power Lockout governance where the machine never arbitrarily restarts without the worker's manual approval (physical key/authentication).
 * Mitigating Misrecognition through Complex Sensors and Local Multimodal Analysis — To compensate for the vulnerabilities of single optical sensor methods, a 60GHz FMCW radar, 32x24 thermal camera, and copper foil capacitance sensor, along with lightweight local AI models for vision and abnormal acoustic detection, are applied simultaneously to precisely distinguish between machined materials and the worker's body.
 * Guaranteeing Air-Gap and Offline Independent Operation — Since it operates with an independent power source and local computation unit regardless of the external network connection status, it fully maintains its safety assistance function even in environments with communication loss or network failures.
3. 5mm Offset Auxiliary Finger, Immediate Upward Recovery, and Quiet Assist Control Mechanisms
 * 5mm Offset Auxiliary Finger Structure — In the area within 5mm of the V-die, compensating for the clearances of ISO 13857 and ISO 13854 standards, an auxiliary finger utilizing the elasticity of a 1.5T spring is deployed to simultaneously perform physical support and pushing functions, physically mitigating the path of the worker's body parts directly entering the hazardous die zone.
 * Quiet Assist Responding to Shear Manual Micro-Machining under 10mm and Existing Device Bypass — Even when existing safeguards are bypassed during the cutting of small pieces or remnants under 10mm on shears and press brakes, this system links with the Special-Purpose Tooling Mode. The triple sensors (thermal 36-degree deviation, FMCW radar scattering angle, SCL capacitance) and lightweight AI micro-track the distance between the worker's fingers and the material being cut in millimeter units. Only when a finger directly enters the Point of Operation does it trigger the 0.1ms eFPGA motor stop and immediate upward recovery, simultaneously satisfying process continuity and safety assistance.
 * Smooth Decelerated Descent Linkage, Ultra-Low Latency Cutoff, and Immediate Upward Recovery Logic to Safe Height (Origin) — Linked with the EN 12622 slow bending speed (Safe Speed below 10mm/s and Mute Point) and the ISO 13855 approach speed calculation formula, the moment an abnormal symptom or a worker's dangerous posture is detected during the smoothly descending section, the motor drive is cut off with triple AND + eFPGA 0.1ms ultra-low latency. Immediately upon cutoff, the upper die is reversely driven upward (Ramp-Up) to a set safe height (origin or user-set release height), fundamentally mitigating secondary pinch accidents caused by pressing inertia.
   * Physical Buffering: Smooth decelerated descent below the Mute Point and T-Reg 15% limitation absorb inertia and hydraulic shock (kinetic energy attenuated by 1/400) and micro-bounces, preventing motor/hydraulic jamming during sudden stops, securing immediate origin upward recovery driving force, and mitigating secondary pinching and die damage.
   * Psychological Buffering: Suppresses the worker's Startle Reflex with a predictable descent speed, preventing sudden movements and assisting in maintaining a stable posture.
   * Ergonomic Buffering (Occupational Disease Prevention): Flexibly attenuates the risk of musculoskeletal disorders and chronic occupational diseases caused by strong mechanical shocks and vibrations fully absorbed by the worker's body during long-term repetitive work, thereby assisting the worker's long-term health rights and work sustainability.
 * 5mm Proximity Bending Exception Handling Safety Assist — Avoids unconditional blanket cutoff upon entering within 5mm of the V-die. The moment the upper die descends, the triple sensors (60GHz scattering angle 30-40 degrees, 32x24 thermal 36 degrees, SCL capacitance) determine in real-time whether the worker's hand/finger position and posture are safe supporting postures (side support, pushing posture, etc.). If determined to be a safe posture, operation is continuously maintained to preserve productivity; if the posture deviates during descent or rapidly enters the hazard zone, ultra-low latency stop and origin upward recovery are executed.
 * Alarm Fatigue Mitigation and Die Shape-Linked Differentiated Risk Assessment Logic — Conforming to the ANSI B11.3 risk assessment guidelines, to prevent Nuisance Trips where simple optical sensors misjudge even normal working postures as hazards during special-shaped die work, the risk is differentially assessed by recognizing the forming profile shapes of the upper punch and lower die (gooseneck, offset blade, etc.), the clearance between the upper and lower dies, and the stroke range. By suppressing false cutoffs when the body is fixed in a safe clearance position that does not intersect the actual pinch line, it induces the safety device to remain constantly active (ON), paradoxically preventing the worker from completely neutralizing the safety device.
 * Dual Operation Mode Governance (Normal Safety Mode vs. Special-Purpose Tooling Mode) — Regardless of the production quantity, this system separates the control layer into two operation modes based on the die profile used and micro-machining conditions.
   * Normal Safety Mode — Activated during standard flat iron and general right-angle bending/shearing operations, performing strict physical protection through triple sensor fusion and triple AND eFPGA interlocks.
   * Special-Purpose Tooling Mode — Activated during all operations applying non-standard/special-shaped dies (gooseneck, offset blade, diagonal blade, oblique dies, etc.), shear manual micro-cutting under 10mm, and 5mm proximity bending, regardless of production quantity. Entry is only possible through UWB smart tag/security watch authentication or dedicated physical key manipulation. Safety and field workability are simultaneously secured through the complex linkage of the 5mm offset auxiliary finger, T-Reg 15% output limitation, die shape-linked risk weighting, independent auxiliary operation when existing safeguards are bypassed, and immediate origin upward recovery control upon posture deviation.
 * Linkage with soma-moa Charter No. 0 and Modern Reinterpretation Safety Framework (Safety-II & Just Culture) — Inheriting the axiom that "System governance is the main, but to human primary work, it is an auxiliary," it occasionally assists in checking the worker's status and establishes a secure operational environment foundation.
   * Safety-II Resilience (Hollnagel Resilience) — Rather than being buried only in accident prevention, it simultaneously achieves production efficiency and safety by focusing on assisting the continuity of 9,999 normal operations stably performed in the field.
   * Just Culture and PII 10-Second Destruction — While citing the Heinrich 300:29:1 ratio as a preventive motive, logs of daily movements and minor familiarity lapses are permanently destroyed from the volatile buffer (RAM 3.2KB) within 10 seconds to prevent and mitigate indiscriminate surveillance misunderstandings. Only critical hazard delta logs are locally preserved in an anonymized CBOR format to establish an autonomous safety culture.
   * Quiet Haptic Alerts — Minimizes worker fatigue by utilizing security watch haptic notifications (1st warning / 2nd stop) recognized only by the worker themselves, instead of public noise alarms.
 * Fail-Safe Defaults and Risk Assessment Provisos — The system's default setting maintains strict Fail-Safe protection. Control governance is established such that die shape-linked exception weighting and the Special-Purpose Tooling Mode are activated only when preceded by third-party verification compliant with relevant safety standards (ISO 13849/16092, ANSI B11.19), certification body risk assessments, or strict parameter approval procedures by a manager.
4. Overcoming Physical Constraints, Ultra-Low Latency Motor Stop, and T-Reg Governance
 * eFPGA-Based Ultra-Low Latency Motor Stop Control — Upon detecting hazard symptoms, it controls the motor enable signal at the hardware circuit level without passing through software threads, immediately inducing machine drive stop at an 0.1ms level execution speed.
 * T-Reg 15% Fail-PRELOCK Mode — Upon intrusion into the sensor detection boundary zone or activation of the Special-Purpose Tooling Mode, machine operating output is forcibly degraded to a 15% level to mitigate shocks from sudden operation.
 * HORIZONTAL_HANDOVER and 80% Proactive PRELOCK — Performs horizontal handovers when moving between multiple work zones, and triggers a proactive PRELOCK when reaching 80% of the hazard threshold to suppress risk proliferation.
 * Anonymous Logging and PII Destruction within 10 Seconds — Upon event occurrence, it generates anonymized logs in CBOR format excluding Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and permanently destroys the raw data within 10 seconds of recording to maintain security.
